# Third-party provenance

The implementation in this initial checkpoint is original. No source units from the community integrations reviewed in the design research have been copied into either product.

The modules depend on and exercise public OpenClaw SDK/runtime APIs (MIT) and Home Assistant APIs (Apache-2.0). Runtime dependencies retain their respective licenses; these notices do not relicense them. Framework source used for investigation is not bundled in either module.

Prior art and potential reuse candidates, exact reviewed revisions, licenses, and architectural exclusions are recorded in [the research review](planning/06-RESEARCH-REUSE-AND-LICENSE-REVIEW.md). Selective reuse remains possible, but any future copied unit must acquire its own attribution/license record before commit.
